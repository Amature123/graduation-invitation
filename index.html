My story

The Journey

Four years, in a few pages

Before I invite you, let me tell you how I got here, and who walked with me.

01The first year

Figuring it out

I started out not knowing where anything was: the classrooms, the shortcuts, or honestly, myself. So I said yes to almost everything. New streets, new people, long rides across a city that slowly began to feel like home.

02What I learned

Keep climbing

Not everything worth learning was in a textbook. I learned to keep going when the rain won't stop, to stay curious, to fail, laugh about it, and get back up. And I learned that the summit is always better reached together.

03The people

A table full of brothers

To my roommates: the grill still smoking past midnight, talking about everything and nothing. You turned four walls into a home.

04After dark

Nights by the river

The nights out by the river, when the city lights made every worry feel a little smaller. Some of my best lessons came after dark, standing next to all of you.

05Along the way

The ones who believed

To the mentors and the wider community who opened doors, gave advice I didn't know I needed, and believed in me before I did.

06Where it all began

Family

And to my family, who were there from the very first day, long before any of this began. Every step of this journey has your footprints in it.

Four years taught me that no one graduates alone.

So I'd love to celebrate with the people who made it possible.
